Privacy Policy for Our Apps and Games

By downloading, installing, and using our apps and games (operated by DayMoon Studio), you agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this Privacy Policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience.

1. Personal Information and Data Collection

We respect your privacy. Our studio does not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, age, or physical address on our servers.

However, we utilize third-party services to enhance gameplay, serve advertisements, and analyze game performance. These third-party services may collect anonymous device-related data.

2. Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Our apps and games integrate Google AdMob to serve advertisements. To comply with privacy regulations globally—including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK)—we utilize the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK as our certified Consent Management Platform (CMP).

2.1 IAB TCF Framework and User Consent

For users located within the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the Google UMP SDK interfaces with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) v2.2. When you start our game, a consent banner will appear allowing you to:

  • Consent: Allow Google and its verified ad technology providers to store/access information on your device and process your data to serve personalized advertisements.
  • Do Not Consent: Decline personalized advertising. In this scenario, Google AdMob will automatically adjust to serve only Limited Ads or Non-Personalized Ads. These ads do not use mobile advertising identifiers but may use contextual information (such as content or general location) to maintain ad delivery.
  • Manage Options: Customize your preferences vendor-by-vendor or purpose-by-purpose.

2.2 Privacy Options and Changing Your Mind

In compliance with regulatory requirements, users can change or withdraw their consent decisions at any time. You can easily reopen the consent preferences by navigating to the "Privacy Settings" button located within the in-game Settings menu. Changing your choice will instantly re-apply the corresponding ad personalization filters.

3. Google Analytics and Advertising Features

Our games integrate Google Analytics to gather anonymous, aggregated game-experience metrics (such as levels cleared, mission scores, and session duration). This helps us optimize and update the game for the future.

By using our services, you acknowledge the collection of data via advertising cookies and anonymous device identifiers. This data contains absolutely no personal identifiers. You can opt out via Google's available opt-out tools at any time.

4. Other Third-Party Plugins (Facebook & Pollfish)

  • Facebook Plugins: If you use the "Share" button in our games, the app may communicate with Facebook Inc. to post your achievements on your wall. We do not store or collect your Facebook credentials or personal profile data.
  • Pollfish SDK: This application may integrate the Pollfish SDK for market research surveys. Pollfish may automatically check your device data, including your mobile advertising ID and installed application metadata, solely to determine survey eligibility under strict compliance with Google Play Store policies. For details, please see the Pollfish Respondent Terms.
