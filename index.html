The Application implements Google AdMob as the exclusive third-party advertising network provider. To serve targeted and contextual advertisements, Google AdMob may automatically access, collect, and process specific non-sensitive data, including:

  • Device hardware identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID for Android or IDFA for iOS devices).

Google evaluates this data to tailor advertisements and audit continuous system delivery performance. For extensive information regarding data management, please inspect the official Google Privacy & Terms.
